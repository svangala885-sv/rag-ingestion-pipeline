# Data Retention Policy

All content in this folder is fictional and was written for this demo. "Harborview Analytics" is an invented company.

## Scope
This policy applies to every dataset stored in the Harborview Analytics lakehouse, including raw landing zones, curated tables and analyst sandboxes.

## Retention periods
Raw landing data is kept for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Curated Silver and Gold tables are kept for seven years to meet audit requirements. Analyst sandbox tables are deleted after 30 days of inactivity.

## Legal hold
When the legal team places a dataset on legal hold, automated deletion is suspended until the hold is released in writing. Contact the records office at records@example.com or 555-010-0199.
