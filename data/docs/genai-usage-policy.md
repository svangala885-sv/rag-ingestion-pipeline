# Generative AI Usage Policy

## Approved tools
Employees may use only the company's approved, enterprise-hosted language models. Public consumer chatbots must not receive company data.

## Sensitive data
Personal data, health information and customer identifiers must be removed or masked before text is sent to any language model. Retrieval systems index only documents classified as internal or public.

## Human review
Model outputs used in customer communication or regulatory filings must be reviewed and approved by a person before release.
